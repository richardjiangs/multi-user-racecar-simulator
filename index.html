The last RX-7 and the last twin-rotor road car anyone sold. There is no crankshaft, no connecting rod, no piston and no valve in the 13B-REW: two rotors turn in two epitrochoid housings on one eccentric shaft, which spins three times for every turn the rotors make. That is why it revs to 8,000 without ever sounding strained, why it weighs 1,270 kg, and why it sounds like nothing else here. The turbos are sequential — the small primary alone to about 4,500 rpm, then the second one joins it. Z puts back the 180 km/h limiter every RX-7 sold in Japan left the factory with. Its circuit here is Okayama.
